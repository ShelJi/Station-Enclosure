Differences from the sketch

  • The frame is 65 cm tall, not the labelled 50 cm. A 40 cm suction motor does not fit under the top row in 50 cm, so the lower section grew by 15 cm. The top row, roof cap, width and floor depth are unchanged.
  • The suction bay is now 44.3 cm tall (4.3 cm above the motor). The water tank bay grew to 28.6 cm and the jetting motor bay to 15.5 cm, keeping the drawn wall thicknesses.
  • The back face leans less steeply: it still goes from 23 cm deep at the floor to 17 cm at the roof seam, but over 55 cm instead of 40 cm.
  • The motors and water tank rest on their bay floors instead of the 1.7–4.6 cm gaps the sketch shows.
  • The Pi 5 is drawn floating mid-bay. It is mounted against the sloped roof underside, the only surface it can reach.
  • Materials are assumed. The pencil sketch shows no finishes.
